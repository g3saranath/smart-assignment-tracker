# Physics Homework 3

Answer the following questions. Show your reasoning where relevant.

1. State Newton's second law of motion and write its equation.

2. A 2 kg block is pushed with a net force of 10 N across a frictionless
   surface. What is its acceleration?

3. Explain the difference between speed and velocity in one or two sentences.

4. A car accelerates uniformly from rest to 20 m/s in 5 seconds. Calculate
   its acceleration and the distance travelled during this time.

5. Briefly describe what kinetic energy is and give its formula.
